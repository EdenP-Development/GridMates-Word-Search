# November 24, 2026 — Book Collection direction

The owner confirmed a matching earned album for each Word Search book and optional activity collections. This pass records that decision in the [Collection design](../design/WS16D_COLLECTION_HOME_SHORTCUT_AND_THEMED_GALLERY_ROADMAP_2026-11-09.md#book-albums-and-activity-collections) and updates the active handoff. [Draft #195](https://github.com/PlayerEden/GridMates-Word-Search/pull/195)

Book albums use milestones from their own book. A Library book and the matching Collection album share the same earned state. Activity collections can reward Daily puzzles, Quick Play variety and other explicit challenges. Use eligible saved completions to award newly available milestones once per item. Garden is the first prototype, with art candidates reviewed against the latest Sudoku revisions at Word Search display sizes.
