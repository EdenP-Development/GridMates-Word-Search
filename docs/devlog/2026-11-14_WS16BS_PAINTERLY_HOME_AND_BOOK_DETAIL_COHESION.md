## Design guardrails and limitations
- Freeze accepted walnut cabinet, shelves, green paging controls and physical covers; no new overlay behind puzzle letter grid.
- The richer texture is a QA-only material, not an authorized production release asset. No user-facing license or commercial rights approval inferred.
- Large system text at 200%, low-end phones, performance, physical full Library paging, actual save/relaunch and completed-book recovery still need on-device verification; Android test compilation is not device execution.
- The new painted paper may look better or too mottled at the owner's actual display brightness; judge from side-by-side v76 and v77 screenshots, and revert/adjust *only that material* if the improvement is not real.
